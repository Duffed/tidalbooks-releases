# TIDAL Books

An unofficial Android app for listening to audiobook albums on TIDAL, with saved listening progress, bookmarks, and a sleep timer.

## Downloads

Download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/Duffed/tidalbooks-releases/releases).

Android 8 or later and your own TIDAL account with an eligible subscription are required.

## Support development

The app is free. If you find it useful, you can support its development with a voluntary contribution via PayPal.

[Support development on PayPal](https://paypal.me/rest0ck)

## About

This repository hosts releases of TIDAL Books. This is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TIDAL. The unofficial integration may change or stop working.
